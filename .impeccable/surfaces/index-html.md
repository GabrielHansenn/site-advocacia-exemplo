# Surface: landing page (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: law-firm owners judging the demo; simulated visitor seeking guidance on a phone. Action: talk via WhatsApp or e-mail. Direction pinned by the brief (palette, sections, dark hero); concept-seed not run because the brief pins the direction.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The firm as a gilt-tooled codex: dark leather ground, gold fillet rules and Caslon lettering, like the bound legal volumes of a traditional practice. Refuses the split-hero-plus-six-cards law template and the gavel/scales cliché.

OWN-WORLD: #14100C leather-dark fields, #D7A042 used only as foil (thin double fillets, small marks, the primary button), stone/parchment light sections #F8F6F2 and #EFE9DF, warm grey #A8A29B. Libre Caslon Display for headings, Hanken Grotesk for text. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows beyond one soft lift on the photo frame.

STORY: Visitor understands who the firm is and its tone (sober, personal), scans the six areas as a codex index, meets the people, and opens a conversation on WhatsApp with the area pre-filled.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark field. Left ~7/12: large Caslon headline over three lines, short description, gold primary CTA plus text-link secondary. Right ~5/12: tall photo of a lamplit colonnade inside a gold double-fillet frame offset from the image; the brief's tagline set as an inscription along the frame. Header over the hero, transparent.

FORM: Bookbinding / codex index (brief-pinned). seed: none (unavailable). Signature interaction: the "Áreas de atuação" index rows; choosing one carries the area into the contact form's subject. Motion grammar: one load moment (fillet lines draw in around the hero frame), image clip reveals on enter, everything visible without JS.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
