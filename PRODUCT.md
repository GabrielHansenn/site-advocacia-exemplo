# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML5 + CSS3 + vanilla JS, no build step, no frameworks or UI libraries. Deployed as static files on Vercel (index.html at root). Chosen by the brief.

## Users

Two audiences:
1. Primary (commercial): owners of Brazilian law firms and solo lawyers evaluating this page as a demo of a site they could commission. They judge craft, credibility and how well it fits OAB advertising rules.
2. Simulated end visitor: a person or company owner looking for legal guidance, usually under some worry, reading on a phone, who needs to understand who the firm is, what it handles and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Demonstration landing page for a fictional firm, AUREUM | ADVOCACIA. Success: a lawyer sees it and believes it was made by a specialised branding studio, and the simulated visitor can reach WhatsApp or e-mail in one tap from anywhere on the page.

## Positioning

Institutional, sober, tradition-anchored advocacy with personalised service for people and companies across six areas (Civil, Empresarial, Trabalhista, Família e Sucessões, Imobiliário, Previdenciário).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend. Contact happens via WhatsApp (wa.me) and mailto; the form composes a WhatsApp message and never simulates a successful send.
- All contact data are placeholders, centralised for easy replacement.
- Must follow OAB advertising norms (Provimento 205/2021 spirit): no promises of results, no discounts, no sensationalism, no testimonials, no invented awards, OAB numbers or statistics, no aggressive client capture.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: AUREUM | ADVOCACIA (fictional, must be easy to swap).
- Colours pinned by the brief: gold #D7A042 and brown-black #14100C as the identity; supports #F8F6F2, #EFE9DF, #A8A29B, #FFFFFF.
- Typography: user chose refined alternatives over Playfair/Cormorant + Inter/Manrope (answered 2026-11-23).
- No gavels or scales-of-justice imagery.

## Evidence on Hand

None real. Team members, contact data and address are fictional and marked as demonstration content. Photos are free-licence stock from Pexels, stored locally in assets/images (user approved local hosting of free-licence stock).

## Product Principles

1. Credibility through restraint: sober language, no claims that cannot be proven.
2. One tap to a conversation: every section leads to the contact options without pressure.
3. Easy to hand over: every name, number and address is replaceable in obvious places.
